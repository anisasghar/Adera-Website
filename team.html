The People Behind the Promise

Character. Conviction. Craft.

Our team brings together decades of experience across global investment, operations, and advisory — united by a shared commitment to building businesses of enduring worth.

At Adera Holdings, we believe that the quality of an investment firm is ultimately a reflection of the people within it. We hold ourselves to the same standards we expect of our portfolio companies — rigorous, principled, and relentlessly focused on the long term.

DR

Daniel Roscom

Founder & Chairman

Daniel founded Adera Holdings on the belief that visionary leadership and principled partnership are the true drivers of enduring value. Across a career defined by building and scaling organisations, he has developed a rare instinct for identifying opportunity, cultivating talent, and guiding businesses through every stage of growth. A natural leader and strategic innovator, Daniel approaches every venture not as a transaction, but as a long-term commitment to the people and ideas he believes in. His conviction — that lasting value is built through trust, vision, and unwavering integrity — is the foundation upon which Adera Holdings stands.

AA

Anis Asghar

Chief Executive Officer

With over 25 years of experience across public and private markets, Anis has built a distinguished career at the intersection of strategic investment, corporate governance, and value creation. He oversees a diversified portfolio spanning direct investments in infrastructure and technology, as well as venture capital, bringing rigorous analytical discipline and long-term conviction to every decision. Anis has chaired investment committees, forged global partnerships with leading institutional investors, and applied a seasoned M&A expertise to lead the acquisition of many of Adera’s core assets. A trusted steward of capital and governance, he combines operational depth with a principled approach that defines the culture and ambition of Adera Holdings.

MO

Marcus Obi

Chief Investment Officer

Marcus oversees Adera's investment strategy, origination, and portfolio construction. His background spans infrastructure finance, growth equity, and technology investment across Europe and Sub-Saharan Africa. He is known for his ability to identify structural tailwinds before they are widely recognised — and for his conviction that the best investments are those made with both financial and human capital in mind.

CW

Claire Whitmore

Head of Operations

Claire ensures that Adera operates to the highest standards of governance, process, and financial integrity. With a background in audit, compliance, and operational transformation at major financial institutions, she is the backbone of Adera's internal discipline. She believes that excellence in operations is not a constraint on ambition — it is what makes ambition sustainable over the long term.
