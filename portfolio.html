Where We Invest

Sectors of Conviction

We invest in businesses and assets where we have deep domain expertise, long-term structural tailwinds, and the conviction to be patient owners.

Adera Holdings maintains a deliberately concentrated portfolio — favouring depth of understanding over breadth of exposure. Each sector we invest in is one where we believe we can add genuine value as partners, not simply as capital providers.

Our Investment Philosophy

We are long-term, concentrated investors. We do not chase trends or rotate through fashionable themes. We invest where we have conviction, hold for the long run, and work actively with our portfolio companies to build businesses of enduring worth. Capital preservation is not a constraint — it is a discipline.
