Financial Services

Financial services sits at the foundation of the global economy. As the conduit through which capital is allocated, risk is priced, and wealth is preserved and transferred across generations, the sector offers a distinctive combination of recurring income, structural growth, and long-term value creation — characteristics well-suited to our investment approach.

Our financial services portfolio spans the breadth of the sector: banking, insurance, wealth management, alternative investments, and the emerging class of technology-enabled financial businesses that are reshaping how services are delivered and consumed. We bring to this sector not only capital but a network of relationships and an understanding of regulatory and competitive dynamics built over many years of active involvement.

Established institutions

We hold strategic minority stakes in a number of well-established financial institutions across banking, insurance, and wealth management. These are businesses with durable competitive positions — strong balance sheets, loyal customer relationships, and brands built over decades. Our approach in these investments is to be a long-term, engaged shareholder: we do not seek operational control, but we do bring active partnership — contributing to strategic thinking, governance, and the networks that open doors to new opportunities.

In banking, we focus on institutions with disciplined underwriting, differentiated client relationships, and demonstrated ability to generate returns through the cycle. In insurance, we are attracted to businesses with technical underwriting expertise and conservative reserving — qualities that compound quietly over time and are often underappreciated by shorter-horizon investors. In wealth management, we see a structural growth opportunity as rising global prosperity, particularly in emerging markets, creates sustained demand for sophisticated financial advisory services.

"The most valuable financial businesses are built on trust — with clients, with regulators, and with the communities they serve. That trust, once established, compounds as reliably as any balance sheet."

Next-generation financial businesses

Alongside our holdings in established institutions, we are actively backing the next generation of financial businesses — those leveraging technology, data, and new distribution models to serve customers that legacy institutions have historically underserved or underpriced. This includes fintech platforms operating across payments, lending, insurance distribution, and investment management, as well as businesses building infrastructure for the alternative investment industry.

We are not passive observers of the disruption underway in financial services. We invest in businesses where we believe the founders and management teams have genuine insight into unmet needs, where the regulatory pathway is navigable, and where the business model has the potential to scale with discipline. In several cases, our existing relationships with established financial institutions have allowed us to create productive connections between our portfolio companies — an example of how a diverse, interconnected portfolio can generate value that no single investment could produce alone.

The alternative investment industry also represents a significant area of focus. As institutional and high-net-worth capital continues to shift towards private markets, the businesses that provide access, administration, and intelligence to that capital are growing in both scale and strategic importance. We hold positions in several such businesses and expect this area of the portfolio to remain active over the years ahead.
