Responsible Investing

How We Invest Matters

Integrity is at the centre of our name, our values, and our investment process. We believe that responsible investing and strong returns are not in conflict — they are inseparable.

At Adera Holdings, responsible investing is not a compliance exercise — it is a core discipline. We integrate environmental, social, and governance considerations into every stage of our investment process, from initial screening to ongoing portfolio management.

E

Environmental

Planet & Resources

We assess the environmental footprint of every business we consider — from energy consumption and emissions to resource efficiency and climate resilience. We actively work with portfolio companies to reduce their environmental impact and identify the long-term risks and opportunities that environmental change creates.

We believe that businesses built for a low-carbon future will outperform over the long term. Environmental thinking is not a constraint on returns — it is a source of them.

S

Social

People & Communities

Capital exists to serve people. We evaluate how each business treats its employees, engages with its communities, and manages its relationships with customers and suppliers. A business that looks after its people and earns genuine trust from its stakeholders is a more durable business.

We encourage our portfolio companies to invest in their workforce, support diversity and inclusion, and consider the broader social impact of what they do — because we believe purpose-driven businesses create lasting value.

G

Governance

Structure & Accountability

Good governance is the foundation of every successful investment. We expect the businesses we back to maintain robust board structures, transparent financial reporting, clear lines of accountability, and strong internal controls. We hold ourselves to the same standards we expect of others.

We believe that governance is not bureaucracy — it is the architecture of trust. Companies with strong governance attract better talent, command greater stakeholder confidence, and deliver more consistent performance over time.

Our Commitment

Adera Holdings is committed to being a responsible steward of capital — not just for the benefit of our partners and portfolio companies, but for the communities, environments, and generations that our investments touch. We recognise that private investment carries real responsibility, and we take that responsibility seriously.

We continuously review and improve our approach to responsible investing, engaging with our portfolio companies on ESG matters and seeking to raise standards across our portfolio over time. We believe the firms that take this seriously today will be the firms that endure.

How We Apply It

ESG Across Our
Investment Process

01

Screening

Before we invest, we identify material ESG risks and opportunities alongside financial assessment. We will not invest in businesses whose practices fundamentally conflict with our values.

02

Due Diligence

Our due diligence process evaluates governance structures, workforce practices, environmental exposure, and regulatory compliance — informing both our investment decision and our post-investment priorities.

03

Ownership

As active partners, we work with management teams to improve ESG performance over the ownership period — setting targets, sharing best practice, and measuring progress.

04

Reporting

We maintain transparent records of ESG activity across our portfolio and hold ourselves accountable for continuous improvement. We believe that what gets measured gets managed.
