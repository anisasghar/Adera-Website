Adera Holdings is a London-based private investment company that acquires, develops, and manages a curated portfolio of businesses and assets across multiple high-conviction sectors. Adera /ah-DEH-rah/  ·  Amharic

A profound, enduring promise — bound by integrity, trust, and unwavering responsibility.

Rooted in ancient Ethiopian culture, Adera is more than a pledge. It is a sacred covenant — carried out with heartfelt dedication, loyalty, and purpose. To give your Adera is to commit not just your words, but your character.

Adera transcends the individual. It is communal: your word serves others, upholds shared values, and strengthens the collective. In this tradition, a promise is never merely personal — it ripples outward, touching lives beyond your own.

This is the spirit that animates everything we do. At Adera Holdings, our word is our bond. Our commitments are carried through with the full weight of that ancient understanding — that true dedication has the power to uplift not just one person, but an entire community.

Our Mission

Adera Holdings is a private investment company built on a single enduring principle: that capital, managed with integrity, has the power to create lasting value. Through disciplined investment, prudent oversight, and a steadfast commitment to stewardship, we exist to generate enduring opportunity — for our partners, our portfolio companies, and the generations that follow.
