Infrastructure

Infrastructure occupies a distinctive position within a long-term portfolio. Essential assets — those upon which economies and populations genuinely depend — generate predictable, inflation-linked cash flows over very long time horizons. For a permanent capital investor, this combination of durability, real-return protection, and low correlation to financial markets is deeply compelling.

Adera Holdings has developed a focused infrastructure portfolio concentrated in assets that are genuinely essential: the kind of infrastructure that societies cannot easily replicate, replace, or do without. Our approach is to invest in high-quality assets with long concession or regulatory frameworks, strong cash generation, and contractual protections that preserve the real value of returns over time. We are not infrastructure traders. We are infrastructure owners.

Transport and energy

Transport infrastructure — airports, ports, toll roads, and rail — represents one of the most mature and well-understood areas of the infrastructure asset class. These are assets with natural monopoly characteristics, high barriers to entry, and demand profiles that have historically proven resilient across economic cycles. We hold interests in transport infrastructure across several jurisdictions, with a focus on assets that serve structurally growing trade corridors or population centres.

In energy infrastructure, our focus has shifted materially towards the transition — the network of assets required to generate, transmit, store, and distribute clean energy at scale. Renewable generation assets, electricity transmission networks, battery storage facilities, and green hydrogen infrastructure are all areas of active interest. The energy transition is one of the largest infrastructure investment programmes in history, and we believe patient, long-duration capital is particularly well-suited to fund it. We are equally attentive to the continued need for reliable conventional energy infrastructure during the transition period, and we do not allow the pace of decarbonisation ambition to exceed the pace of available technology.

"Essential infrastructure is the bedrock of civilised life. Owning it with a long time horizon, through the right structures, is one of the most reliable ways to preserve and grow capital across generations."

Digital infrastructure and water

Digital infrastructure has emerged as one of the defining infrastructure themes of our era. Data centres, fibre networks, mobile tower portfolios, and subsea cable systems are now as essential to economic activity as roads and power grids — and they share many of the same investment characteristics: high barriers to entry, long asset lives, contracted revenues, and demand that is structurally increasing. We invest across the digital infrastructure spectrum, from core data centre campuses serving hyperscale cloud providers to edge facilities supporting the latency requirements of emerging AI and autonomous systems applications.

Water is perhaps the most essential infrastructure category of all. Water utilities, treatment facilities, irrigation networks, and water technology businesses — those improving efficiency, quality, or access to water resources — represent a long-term investment conviction for us that is grounded in both financial and real-world logic. Water scarcity is not a future risk; it is a present reality in many parts of the world, and the infrastructure required to address it represents a multi-decade investment need. We approach water infrastructure with the same long-duration, essential-asset discipline that characterises the rest of our infrastructure portfolio, and we view it as among the most structurally durable investment exposures available to a permanent capital investor.
