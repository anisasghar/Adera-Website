Real Estate

Real estate has long been a pillar of enduring wealth. Adera Holdings approaches the asset class with a long-duration perspective — identifying premium locations across global gateway cities where supply constraints, institutional demand, and structural population dynamics combine to support sustained value appreciation over time.

Our real estate strategy is not defined by opportunistic trading. We seek assets of genuine quality: well-located, well-constructed, and capable of generating reliable income across market cycles. We hold with conviction, manage actively, and expect to own many of our real estate positions for decades rather than years.

United Kingdom

London occupies a singular position in global real estate. As one of the world's foremost financial and cultural capitals, it combines deep institutional demand, a chronic structural undersupply of prime property, and an enduring appeal to international capital that few cities can match. These conditions create a compelling long-term environment for patient investment.

Our UK interests are concentrated in central London, where we hold positions across prime residential and commercial property. In the residential segment, we focus on established prime and super-prime neighbourhoods — locations characterised by architectural scarcity, irreplaceable addresses, and a buyer base that is genuinely global. These assets have historically demonstrated resilience through economic cycles, underpinned by constrained new supply and consistent demand from domestic and international purchasers of significant means.

On the commercial side, we are invested in mixed-use developments and institutional-grade office and retail assets across central London. We prioritise assets with long-term lease income secured against creditworthy tenants — structures that generate predictable cash flows and are well-suited to our patient capital approach. We are also attentive to the ongoing repositioning of London's commercial stock, where older buildings are being fundamentally reimagined as mixed-use destinations combining workspace, residential, and amenity — a trend we expect to generate attractive opportunities over the medium term.

United States

The United States offers both the world's deepest real estate markets and some of its most compelling structural narratives. We concentrate our US exposure across three primary cities — New York, Miami, and Los Angeles — each offering distinct characteristics and opportunity sets within a broadly favourable long-term framework.

In New York, our focus is on trophy commercial real estate and luxury residential developments in Manhattan. The trophy office market in particular has bifurcated sharply in recent years: while commodity office space faces structural challenges, best-in-class buildings in prime locations continue to command premium rents and maintain high occupancy — a dynamic we expect to persist as occupiers concentrate in quality. We also hold positions in luxury residential developments, where New York's global appeal and constrained supply at the top end of the market provide durable support.

Miami has undergone a structural transformation, evolving from a regional market into a genuine international gateway city over the past decade. The influx of financial services firms, technology companies, and high-net-worth individuals has created sustained demand across commercial and residential segments. In Los Angeles, we are invested in luxury residential and in the logistics and warehousing sector — the latter driven by the continuing growth of e-commerce and the constraints of Southern California's port infrastructure, which structurally limits the supply of well-located last-mile distribution assets.

"The value of real estate is ultimately a function of location, scarcity, and time. We invest where all three converge — and we hold long enough to let them work."

Asia

Asia represents one of the most significant long-term opportunities in global real estate, driven by the sustained growth of an affluent and urbanising middle class, the continued deepening of financial systems across the region, and the expansion of Asia's high-net-worth population. Our Asia real estate portfolio is concentrated in three of the region's most established and liquid markets: Singapore, Hong Kong, and Tokyo.

Singapore's real estate market is characterised by its stability, transparency, and consistent demand from both domestic and international investors. We hold positions in premium residential towers and high-quality commercial assets in the city-state's central business district, where supply is tightly controlled and demand from multinational tenants remains robust. Singapore's status as the region's preeminent wealth management hub provides particular support for the high-end residential sector.

In Hong Kong, we maintain selective exposure to prime residential and retail destinations in established locations, where long-term scarcity of land and the enduring density of the city underpin values across cycles. Tokyo, by contrast, offers a distinctive opportunity in Asia — a large, liquid, and institutionally developed market where long-term demographic shifts are being offset by urban densification, international capital inflows, and a renewed focus on redeveloping ageing commercial stock into mixed-use commercial destinations. We view our Asia real estate positions collectively as an expression of conviction in the long-term trajectory of Asian wealth creation and urbanisation.
