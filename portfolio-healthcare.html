Healthcare

Healthcare is one of the most structurally compelling long-term investment sectors available. Demographic inevitability — the sustained ageing of populations across developed and emerging markets alike — creates a demand backdrop that is independent of economic cycles. Layered over this is a period of remarkable scientific progress: the convergence of genomics, data science, and precision medicine is beginning to change what is possible in human health in ways that will play out over decades.

Adera Holdings has built a healthcare portfolio that reflects both of these themes. We invest across the sector — from established healthcare services businesses with durable demand profiles to growth-stage life sciences and medical technology companies working at the frontier of what medicine can do. Our approach is consistent: long-term positions, genuine conviction, and active partnership with management teams who are building businesses of enduring significance.

Healthcare services and the ageing population

The demographic case for healthcare services investment is not a projection — it is an observable reality unfolding across every major economy. The proportion of the global population aged over 65 is growing steadily and will continue to do so for the remainder of this century. This creates predictable, growing demand for a wide range of healthcare services: primary care, specialist consultations, diagnostics, elective procedures, rehabilitation, and long-term care. These are not discretionary services. They are needs that will be met regardless of the broader economic environment.

We invest in healthcare services businesses that are well-positioned to serve this growing demand — those with quality clinical outcomes, strong regulatory track records, and operational models that can scale without compromising the standard of care. We are particularly interested in businesses that are bringing technology to bear on the efficiency and accessibility of healthcare delivery: digital health platforms, remote monitoring services, and care coordination businesses that allow the healthcare system to meet rising demand without a proportionate increase in cost.

"The best healthcare investments share a characteristic with the best infrastructure investments: the demand is not created by markets, it is created by human need. That is a foundation on which patient capital can build with confidence."

Life sciences, medical technology, and precision medicine

Beyond services, we invest in businesses operating at the frontier of medical science. Life sciences — encompassing drug discovery, biologics, and cell and gene therapy — is undergoing a transformation driven by advances in genomic sequencing, computational biology, and AI-assisted drug development. The cost of developing new therapeutic modalities has fallen significantly, and the ability to identify and reach the right patients has improved dramatically. We back businesses in this space that have genuine scientific differentiation and management teams with the expertise to navigate the complex regulatory and commercial pathways to market.

Medical technology — the devices, instruments, and implantable systems that diagnose, monitor, and treat disease — is another area of active investment. We are drawn to businesses that are improving clinical outcomes in measurable ways, that have defensible intellectual property, and that are addressing unmet needs in high-volume, high-acuity clinical settings. Diagnostics, in particular, is an area we find compelling: the ability to detect disease earlier, with greater precision, and at lower cost is one of the most direct routes to improving patient outcomes and reducing the overall burden on healthcare systems.

Precision medicine — the practice of tailoring medical treatment to the individual characteristics of each patient, including their genetic profile — represents the long-term direction of travel for the entire sector. We hold investments across the enabling infrastructure of precision medicine: genomic data platforms, biomarker development businesses, and the diagnostics and analytics tools that translate scientific insight into clinical practice. These are investments made with long time horizons and genuine conviction that the transformation underway in medicine will be one of the defining value-creation stories of the coming decades.
